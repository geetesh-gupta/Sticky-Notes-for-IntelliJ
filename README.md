# Sticky-Notes-for-IntelliJ: Your Context-Aware Dev Notepad

Stop switching between your IDE and external note apps. **StickyNotes** brings a lightweight, powerful note-taking experience directly into your workspace, keeping your thoughts where your code is.

### 🚀 Key Features
- **Project-Specific Backlog:** Keep a dedicated scratchpad for every project. No more cluttered `.txt` files in your root directory.
- **Global Search:** Quickly find that "fix this later" note across all your open projects.
- **Smart Tool Window:** A sleek, minimal sidebar to manage your active tasks and reminders without leaving the editor.

### 💡 Why StickyNotes?
As developers, we often find "dirty code" or "refactoring ideas" while working on a different task. StickyNotes allows you to capture those thoughts instantly without breaking your current flow.
